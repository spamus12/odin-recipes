Beef Tacos

Description: Make 4 crispy beef tacos with rich flavor

Equipment required:

  • Cutting board and decently-sized knife
  • Disposable gloves
  • Medium-sized skillet and a stove
  • Spatula
Ingredients:
  • 1 green pepper
  • 1 head of lettuce
  • 1 pound of thawed ground beef
  • 1 tomato
  • Any cooking oil
  • Assorted seasonings
  • Shredded cheddar-jack cheese
  • Taco shells

Once you have your ingredients and cooking items, it's time to prepare your workspace and ingredients

Ingredient preparation:

  1. Wash your hands
  2. Chop the pepper and tomato into small chunks and set them aside
  3. Chop or pull apart the lettuce and set it aside
  4. Drizzle a little oil on your skillet and put on some gloves
  5. Put the thawed ground beef onto your skillet and pull it apart until there are very few chunks left
  6. Sprinkle your chosen seasonings onto the raw beef and use your hands to mix them together
After preparing, it is time to begin cooking.
  1. Set your stove to a medium heat and use your spatula to constantly stir the beef
  2. Continue stirring until all the beef chunks are brown
  3. Distribute the beef evenly between your 4 taco shells and dress them with your lettuce, tomatoes, peppers, and cheese
  4. Enjoy!
