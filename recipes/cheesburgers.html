Cheeseburgers

Description: Make 4 towering monsters of beef and taste.

Equipment required:

  • 2 separate, sterile surfaces (like a plate)
  • Cutting board and decently-sized knife
  • Disposable gloves
  • Medium-sized skillet and a stove
  • Spatula
Ingredients:
  • 1 pound of thawed ground beef
  • 1 head of lettuce
  • 1-2 tomatoes
  • 4 pairs of cheeseburger buns
  • Any cooking oil
  • Assorted seasonings
  • Cheddar cheese, block or sliced
  • Condiments, like ketchup, mustard, and mayonnaise
  • Sliced pickles

Once you have your ingredients and cooking items, it's time to prepare your workspace and ingredients

Ingredient preparation:

  1. Wash your hands
  2. If your cheese is in a block, then cut it into 4 thin slices and set them aside
  3. Cut or pull apart the lettuce and set it aside
  4. Slice the tomato(es), set them aside, and wash your hands
  5. Prepare your separate surface and put on your gloves
  6. Take the ground beef and separate it into 4 equal-sized patties with your hands and set them aside

After preparing, it is time to begin cooking

Cooking instructions:

  1. Heat the stove to a medium temperature and drizzle a little oil onto the skillet
  2. Place one of the patties into the skillet (you could also throw some seasoning on them here)
  3. Making sure to occasionally slide the patty around in the oil, wait until the bottom of the patty is completely brown
  4. After one side is no longer pink, use the spatula to flip the patty over
  5. Repeat this process until the patty is evenly cooked all the way through (there shouldn't be any pink left)
  6. After the patty is cooked all the way through, then place it on another plate to the side
  7. Repeat the process with the rest of the patties until all 4 are fully cooked
  8. Finally, dress your cheeseburgers with some condiments, cheese, lettuce, and tomatoes
  9. Enjoy!
