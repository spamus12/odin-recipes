Cheeseburgers

Description: Make 4 towering monsters of beef and taste.

Equipment required:

  • Cutting board and decently-sized knife
  • Disposable gloves
  • Medium-sized skillet
Ingredients:
  • 1 pound of thawed ground beef
  • 1 head of lettuce
  • 1-2 tomatoes
  • 4 pairs of cheeseburger buns
  • Any cooking oil
  • Assorted seasonings
  • Cheddar cheese, block or sliced
  • Condiments, like ketchup, mustard, and mayonnaise
  • Sliced pickles

Once you have your ingredients and cooking items, it's time to prepare your workspace and ingredients

Ingredient preparation:

  1. Wash your hands
  2. Cut or pull apart the lettuce and set it aside
  3. Slice the tomato(es), set them aside, and wash your hands
  4. Drizzle a little oil onto your skillet
  5. Put the thawed beef into the skillet and, with gloves on, pull it apart until there are very few clumps left
  6. Remove your gloves and add some of your seasonings to the beef
