Pepperoni Pizza

Description: Make a 12-inch pie of fresh pepperoni pizza

Equipment required:

  • A conventional cooking-oven and heat-resistant glove
  • Cheese grater
  • Cutting board and decently-sized knife
  • Pizza-cutter
  • Pizza pan at least 12 inches wide
  • Small rubber brush
Ingredients:
  • 12-inch pizza crust
  • Mozarella cheese (block or shredded)
  • Pepperoni sausage (full or sliced)
  • Tomato sauce

Once you have your ingredients and cooking items, it's time to prepare your workspace and ingredients

Ingredient preparation:

  1. Wash your hands
  2. If your cheese isn't shredded, then use the cheese grater to shred it
  3. If your pepperoni sausage isn't sliced, then cut it into 12-16 thin slices
  4. Lay the pizza crust onto your pizza pan
  5. Pour some sauce onto the crust and use your brush to evenly distribute it
  6. Sprinkle shredded cheese across the pizza, making sure to cover most of the sauce
  7. Lay out your pepperonis across the top of the pizza, evenly spaced
  8. Pre-heat the oven to 350 degrees Fahrenheit
After preparing, it is time to begin cooking.
  1. Using your heat-glove, place the pizza pan on the middle grate in the oven
  2. Every few minutes, check on the pizza as it cooks
  3. Once either the cheese is entirely melted or the outer crust becomes crunchy, turn off the oven
  4. After sitting for roughly 30 seconds, carefully pull the pizza pan out
  5. Let it cool for a few minutes, and then use your pizza cutter to divide the pizza into 6-8 even slices
  6. Enjoy!
